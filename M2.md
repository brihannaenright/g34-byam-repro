## Findings

- **RQ1 (DeepSeek, P8 vs P1):** P8 beat P1 by 2 cases. It gained 067f5d2c and 0abf7148 and lost none. This matches the direction of the paper's finding.
- **RQ2 (Opus vs DeepSeek):** At P1, Opus beat DeepSeek by 2 cases (gained 10d7545c and 0abf7148, lost none). At P8, Opus did worse by a net 2 cases: it gained 10d7545c but lost 0305beaf, 067f5d2c and 16ae40b1, all of which were `ERROR_MODEL_RESPONSE`.
- **RQ3 (Opus, P8 vs P1):** P8 did not beat P1 for Opus. It lost 0305beaf and 16ae40b1 and gained none. That is consistent with the idea that extra context helps less as models get stronger. **But the result is confounded:** both lost cases ended in `ERROR_MODEL_RESPONSE` at P8. Opus returned that error on 6/12 cases at P8 against 2/12 at P1, so the gap reflects model-response failures rather than repair quality.
- **Semantic-risk note:** Opus P1 on 067f5d2c compiled but failed the tests. This is the only case where compilation success and repair success came apart.
